Two organic compounds — magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine and to taurine. Both dissolve in water and taste neutral. The choice of forms is Christian’s; together they come to 40 per cent of the daily reference value.

Magnesium contributes to normal functioning of the nervous system and to normal psychological function.

What’s not in it

  • Melatonin.HESPYRA is not a sleep product.
  • Sugar, sweeteners.The sweetness comes from glycine, an amino acid. Whether that is enough is for development to decide — not for a sweetener.
  • Flavourings.The cocoa and the vanilla are real. Nothing else is added.
  • A “blend”.Every amount is listed on its own. Otherwise “6 g formula” would be a remarkably imprecise piece of information.
